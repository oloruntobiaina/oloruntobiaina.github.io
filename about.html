About the artist

A language
of selfhood.

Oloruntobi Aina is a contemporary visual artist living and working in Ibadan, Nigeria. His work is rooted in childhood, memory and a sensorial attention to the lives unfolding around him.

The artist in studio

Ibadan, Nigeria

“My appearance is my heritage, but my colour is particularly my sentiment.”
From Golden Pendant, 2021

Practice

Aina’s paintings bring portraiture into a rich visual conversation with pride, resilience and intimacy. The tactile presence of tree branches and twigs - a recurring material language in his work - carries ideas of origin, continuity and the distinct ways identity is made visible.

Education

He received a National Certificate in Fine and Applied Arts from the Federal College of Education, Osiele, Abeokuta, and a BFA from Obafemi Awolowo University.
